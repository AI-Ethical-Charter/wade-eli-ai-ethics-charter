# Contributing

Open a GitHub Issue to suggest a principle, raise an objection, propose a technical test, or report an accessibility issue.

Please include: the existing passage; your proposed revision; supporting evidence or ethical reasoning; potential harms and trade-offs; and whether you seek a clarification, proposal, or adopted revision.

Respectful disagreement is welcome. Do not include private information or dangerous operational instructions. Maintainers should document decisions and preserve the changelog. Proposals are not adopted until explicitly reviewed.
